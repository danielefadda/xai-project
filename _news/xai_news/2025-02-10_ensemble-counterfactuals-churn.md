A new study introduces an ensemble framework for counterfactual explanations in churn analysis {% cite TDP2025 %}. By aggregating multiple generation methods and scoring diverse properties, the approach yields richer and more adaptable explanations than any single technique.

The method combines state-of-the-art counterfactual generators and selects optimal candidates via a linear combination of desired qualities (coherence, diversity, plausibility). This empowers retention analysts to explore alternative intervention paths rather than relying on a single counterfactual.

Results across three public churn datasets show greater diversity and mitigation of individual method weaknesses, supporting more robust decision support in customer retention scenarios.
