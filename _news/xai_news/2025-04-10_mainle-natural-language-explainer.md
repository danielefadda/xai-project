MAINLE {% cite SFF2025 %} presents a multi-agent, interactive approach to local explanations expressed in natural language. It coordinates agents to generate conversational, user-aligned explanatory narratives for classification tasks.

The framework advances usability by enabling iterative clarification and tailored perspectives, bridging static explanation formats and dialogue-driven interpretation.

Its multi-agent design opens pathways for adaptive, audience-aware explanation systems integrating reasoning components and linguistic variation.
