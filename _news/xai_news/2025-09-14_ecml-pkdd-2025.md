The XAI project team had a strong presence at **ECML-PKDD 2025** in Oporto, presenting multiple contributions that advance the state of the art in interpretable machine learning.

Key works presented include:
* **SafeGen**: A genetic method for safeguarding privacy and fairness {% cite CMM2025 %}.
* **MASCOTS**: Model-agnostic symbolic counterfactual explanations for time series {% cite PSW2025 %}.
* **GrouX**: Exploring group explainability through local approximation {% cite SGP2025 %}.
* **Forgotten Items**: An interpretable unsupervised approach for retail {% cite CBS2025 %}.

These works demonstrate the project's commitment to privacy, fairness, and interpretability in complex data domains.
