FeDa4Fair {% cite HCC2025 %} delivers synthetic and semi-synthetic client-level datasets to benchmark fairness in federated learning under heterogeneous bias conditions.

The library supports controlled evaluation of mitigation methods across sensitive attribute distributions, enabling reproducible comparisons at both global and per-client levels.

This resource accelerates rigorous fairness experimentation and standardization in privacy-constrained multi-party training.
