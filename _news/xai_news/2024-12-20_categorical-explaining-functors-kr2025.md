The XAI project presents a significant theoretical contribution at the KR 2025 conference with the proposal of a rigorous mathematical framework to ensure coherence and faithfulness in AI explanations.

The paper {% cite FGB2025 %} introduces, using category theory, an explaining functor that structurally preserves logical entailment between explanations and the reasoning of the opaque model. This approach overcomes the limitations of current heuristic methods that often generate contradictory or unfaithful explanations.

Validation on synthetic benchmarks demonstrates how this framework significantly mitigates the generation of inconsistent explanations, paving the way for more reliable and mathematically founded explanation systems.
