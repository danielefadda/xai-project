Research on federated learning explainability introduces FastSHAP++ {% cite BCN2025 %}, an adaptation of FastSHAP that integrates differential privacy to safeguard client data while producing local feature contribution explanations.

The work evaluates privacy–utility trade-offs, comparing centrally generated explanations versus federated private ones across multiple datasets. The method prevents raw training data sharing and reduces leakage risk inherent in classical explanation pipelines.

This contributes to responsible deployment of explainability in settings with strict data sovereignty and heterogeneous client distributions.
