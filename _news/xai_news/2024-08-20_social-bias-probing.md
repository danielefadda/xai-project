The XAI project publishes research {% cite MSG2024 %} at EMNLP 2024 on probing social biases in language models using a novel interpretable approach.

The paper introduces a probing methodology that systematically examines how social biases are encoded in the internal representations of large language models. The approach combines interpretability techniques with bias detection to understand where and how stereotypical associations emerge in model architectures.

The results reveal specific layers and attention mechanisms responsible for encoding biased representations. The study provides insights for developing more fair and transparent language models, highlighting the importance of explainability in understanding and mitigating social biases in AI systems.
