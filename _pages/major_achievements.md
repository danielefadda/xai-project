<div class="lead my-4">
  <p>This page summarizes the major scientific, methodological, and societal achievements of the XAI project from 2019 to 2025. It highlights key milestones that have advanced the field of Explainable Artificial Intelligence (XAI) and contributed to the broader AI research community.</p>
<p>After years of intensive research, the XAI project ("Science and technology for the explanation of AI decision making") has reached fundamental milestones in defining a trustworthy and transparent <strong>Human-Centered AI</strong>. Below are the key results achieved by our consortium (CNR, UniPi, SNS).
</p>
</div>

## 1. A New Vision: Human–AI Coevolution
One of the most significant theoretical outcomes of the project is the formalization of a new line of research on **Human–AI Coevolution**. This paradigm explores how the interaction between humans and intelligent systems reciprocally shapes the behavior of both.

- **Key Publication:** Concept formalized in a paper published in *Artificial Intelligence* (Volume 339, February 2025).
- **Impact:** Now central to the "Human-Centered AI" spoke of the national FAIR (Future AI Research) project and to collaborations with European networks such as **TAILOR** and **Humane-AI-net**.

*Explore more:* See our overview pages on [Research Lines](/r_lines_overview/) and [Events & News](/news/).

## 2. Algorithmic and Methodological Innovations
We have developed and refined a suite of algorithms covering diverse explainability needs:

- **LORE and Logic Rule Evolution:** Local rule-based explanations extended to hybrid decision-making contexts and privacy-aware settings. Results published in *Transactions on Data Privacy* (2025).
- **Explainability for Time Series (LASTS):** Advanced model-agnostic symbolic counterfactual explanations for time series classifiers.
- **Reasoning on Explanations (REASONX):** Introduced the paradigm of **Declarative Reasoning on Explanations**, enabling logical reasoning over AI justifications.
- **GLOCALX:** Merges local explanations into global (MESO-level) views for improved interpretive synthesis.

*Explore more:* Browse the [Selected Publications](/publications_selection/) and full [Publications](/publications/) pages; see code and demos in [Resources](/resources/).

## 3. XAI for Science and Health (XAI4Science)
Towards the end of the project, we applied our technologies to complex scientific problems where explanation is as crucial as prediction.

- **ILLUME4Science (Proof of Concept):** Applied the new **ILLUME (Interpretable Latent Space)** method to biomedical domains (pharmacology, cancer research) to generate novel scientific hypotheses.
- **Healthcare Applications:** Experimental use of XAI methods in post-stroke rehabilitation; work presented at **AI for Science 2025**.

## 4. XAI Library and Open Source Resources
To ensure our research is accessible and reusable:

- **XAI Library:** Consolidated a comprehensive software library with visual interfaces and performance / explanation quality optimizations.
- **Open Source:** All code released under permissive open-source licenses.

*Explore more:* Visit [Resources](/resources/) for code, datasets, and documentation.

## 5. Ethics, Law, and Responsibility
The ethical impact of AI has been a pillar of the project.

- **Thought Leadership:** Hosted international experts (e.g., Prof. Ricardo Baeza-Yates, Prof. James Brusseau) contributing to defining challenges of **Responsible AI**.
- **Research Focus:** Addressed the link between privacy and explainability, and leveraged XAI to uncover discrimination in data.

*Explore more:* Check our [News & Events](/news/) archives.

---

## 6. Empowering the Next Generation
The XAI project has been a talent incubator:

- **Training:** Numerous PhD students and postdocs now working on frontier topics: generative AI for mobility, explainable federated learning, integration of LLMs with XAI.
- **Education:** Established a dedicated PhD course on **Explainable AI** at the *Scuola Normale Superiore*.
