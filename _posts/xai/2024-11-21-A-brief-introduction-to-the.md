<b>Abstract: </b> Group equivariant non-expansive operators (GENEOs) are mathematical tools for approximating data observers when data are represented by real-valued or vector-valued functions (<a href="https://rdcu.be/bP6HV">https://rdcu.be/bP6HV</a>). These operators are based on the assumption that the geometric properties of observers influence data interpretation. In this talk, we will discuss key properties of GENEOs, explore their use in Machine Learning, and examine their potential applications in Explainable Artificial Intelligence. The presentation will be informal, with mathematical details kept to a minimum.
